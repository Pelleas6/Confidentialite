On your device

Games, saves, preferences and diagnostics stay on this device. There are no accounts, ad tracking or automatic diagnostic uploads. Uninstalling erases private app data.

A fingerprint of each ROM’s contents is calculated on your device to group copies of the same game. It stays on the device.

Access to games

With your Android permission, automatic search scans shared storage for games. You can also choose a single file or folder. You can revoke these permissions in Android. Confirming deletion removes the ROM file.

Online cover art

Online covers are optional. Previews and downloads send GitHub or Libretro your IP address and the requested image path, which may include the game title.

Bluetooth controllers

Android handles pairing. During play, Game OverTime receives controller input and does not store the controller’s name.

Notifications · Google Play

Game notifications and the reminder after seven days without playing run on your phone. Usage dates and notification preferences stay on the device. After three days an optional invitation can open Google Play, which handles your review. Android manages screen mirroring to your TV.

Your data and choices

Removing a folder in the app revokes its individual permission. You can turn off all-files access separately in Android settings. Reimporting the same ROM restores access to its save as long as the app’s data is still on the device. You can clear that data in Android settings.
