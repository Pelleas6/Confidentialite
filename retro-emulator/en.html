On your device

Games, saves, preferences and diagnostics stay on this device. There are no accounts, ad tracking or automatic diagnostic uploads. Uninstalling erases private app data.

Access to games

With your Android permission, automatic search scans shared storage for games. You can also choose a single file or folder. You can revoke these permissions in Android. Confirming deletion removes the ROM file.

Online cover art

Online covers are optional. Previews and downloads send GitHub or Libretro your IP address and the requested image path, which may include the game title. External links open in your browser.

Bluetooth controllers

Android handles pairing. During play, Rétro Emulator receives controller input and does not store the controller’s name.

Your data and choices

Removing a folder in the app revokes its individual permission. You can turn off all-files access separately in Android settings. Reimporting the same ROM restores access to its save as long as the app’s data is still on the device. You can clear that data in Android settings.
